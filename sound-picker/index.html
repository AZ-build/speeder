SPEEDER · SOUND PICKER

How to listen: tap RIDE on each card to hear about 12 seconds of a run (shooting, hits, a power-up, an explosion, getting hit, a shield, a crash). Try the phone speaker first, since that's how most friends play, then headphones. Tap a single sound to hear it alone.
iPhone: if you hear nothing, turn off silent mode (the switch or Action button on the side).
Every sound here is free to use (CC0): the current set is from Kenney's packs, the new sets were made from scratch for Speeder. Music: "Synthwave House Loop" by Fupi.
