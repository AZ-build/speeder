    HOW TO PLAY

    Riding

    Steer
    Put your thumb down anywhere and slide left or right. It leans a little as it moves. Near the side rails it banks up on its side and gets skinnier, so tight gaps by the edge are easier to slip through. On a computer, use the arrow keys or A and D.
    Shoot
    Your guns fire on their own, straight ahead. To aim, steer so you’re lined up. In a bend, bullets drift toward the outside.
    Hull
    The pink hexes, top left. Every hit costs one and slows you down for a moment. Lose all three and you’re wrecked.
    Max 3 hull
    Ramps
    Drive onto a green ramp to jump for about a second. You can still steer in the air, and you fly over mines, walls and lasers. Pylons are too tall to jump.

    Power-ups

    Gates come in pairs. Drive through the one you want. The two labels under your speeder tell you what’s coming, and the side you’re lined up with lights up. Sometimes mines guard one gate.

    SHIELD
    Blocks the next hit. A double ring means you have two.
    Max 2. Not offered once you have 2.
    GUNS+
    Lasts the whole run.
    1–4 more guns (1, 2, 4, then 5)
    5 piercing: bullets go through enemies and mines
    6 wing missiles: a homing missile every 1.6 seconds
    7 heavy rounds: double damage
    8 max power: wing missiles twice as often, faster bullets
    Max level 8. Not offered once maxed.
    RAPID
    Your guns fire twice as fast.
    Lasts 12 seconds
    REPAIR
    Fixes one hull hex. If your hull is already full, you get +500 points instead.
    Hull max 3
    MISSILES
    Fires 8 homing missiles. They go after enemies first, then mines, anywhere ahead of you, and the launch wipes out incoming enemy fire.
    Each missile does 3 damage
    2X SCORE
    Every point is doubled. Taking another 2X while it’s running restarts the timer.
    Lasts 15 seconds. Doesn’t stack past 2X.

    Obstacles

    Pylons
    Tall towers. You can’t shoot them or jump them. Steer around.
    Sliding pylons
    Pylons that glide from side to side. The glowing line on the road shows how far they travel.
    Walls
    A barrier with one gap. The arrows on it point to the gap. Bullets can’t get through. Some come in a zig-zag of half-walls.
    Jump barriers
    Cover the whole track and have green up-arrows. The only way past is to take the ramp before it (“HIT THE RAMP”).
    Laser fences
    Red beams across the track with a gap that slides side to side. Follow the blinking yellow post. Bullets pass straight through lasers.
    Mines
    Orange spiked balls. Two shots to pop (one with heavy rounds). Jump over them for an AIR bonus.
    +50 each, AIR +150 per thing you fly over
    Mine snakes
    A line of mines swaying like a snake. Weave through the gaps or shoot a hole.

    Enemies

    Drones
    Hover ahead and shoot at you. Some swoop in from behind.
    3 hits to destroy, +250
    Turrets
    Sit on the road and fire as you get close. Driving into one costs a hit.
    3 hits, +200
    Ram bikes
    Charge straight at you and steer toward you.
    2 hits, +150
    Enemy fire
    Glowing orange balls. Dodge them, or block one with a shield.

    Warp Run

    The new mode. Pick it on the title screen. Classic is still there, untouched, with its own leaderboard.

    Five worlds and world gates
    Each world has its own colors, its own look for pylons and walls, and its own hazard. A world lasts about 40 seconds, however fast you’re going. At the end stands a world gate: you can see the next world through it. Fly through and that world builds itself around you, with a couple of seconds of clear road before the action starts again.
    World cleared: +1,000
    The Warden
    The boss at the end of the fifth world. It weaves across the track, so line up under it to hit it. The fight has three stages, one for each third of its health (the notches on its health bar). It never stops firing single aimed shots, so keep moving. On top of that, first: volleys, walls of mines with a gap, lanes that light up red before they are scorched, and laser fences sent down the road. Then it adds three-way bursts, ram bikes, a second mine wall and a second set of lanes straight after the first. In the last stage the bursts are five wide, the lanes come three sets in a row, and everything is faster.
    +5,000 and a prototype
    Loops
    Beat the boss and the five worlds start again, tougher, and each world a little faster than the last. Every point is multiplied by the loop number: x2 in loop 2, x3 in loop 3, and so on.
    Power cells and Overdrive
    Destroyed enemies drop a green cell. It stays on the road where the enemy went down, so steer over it to collect it. Fill the CELLS bar and your guns go into OVERDRIVE: double fire rate and +1 damage.
    8 cells · lasts 8 seconds
    Close calls
    Slip past a pylon, wall, mine, truck or crystal with almost no room to spare and you score a bonus. Chain them for more. Close calls also charge Overdrive.
    +40, growing to +400 in a chain
    Riding the rail
    Tucking up against a side rail is great for a tight gap, but the rail heats up while you ride it: sparks turn from yellow to red and RAIL HOT flashes. Stay too long and it overloads. You’re thrown back onto the road (no damage) and both rails stay locked while they cool.
    about 3 seconds of riding · 5 seconds to cool
    Prototypes
    After each boss you choose one of two permanent upgrades for the rest of the run:
    HEAVY CORE +1 damage on every bullet (can be taken again)
    TWIN WINGS wing missiles fire in pairs
    HULL +1 a fourth hull hex, fully repaired
    AEGIS a shield regrows every 20 seconds
    MAGNET cells fly to you, Overdrive lasts 12 seconds
    CHAIN BLAST every kill damages what’s near it
    Neon Desert: ram packs
    Wide, fast straights, and ram bikes that come at you in an arrowhead. They hold their formation, so pick a gap or shoot one open.
    Neon City: traffic and tunnels
    Hover-trucks drive the same way you do, only slower. Weave between them. You can’t shoot them.
    Sky Islands: chasms and wind
    Where the road is missing, a full-width ramp throws you across. When WIND shows under your speeder, a crosswind is about to push you sideways: steer against it.
    Crystal Wastes: crystals
    A glowing crack in the road means a crystal is about to burst out of it. Dodge it or shatter it.
    3 hits, +100
    Cosmic Highway: meteors
    An orange ring on the road is where a meteor is about to land. It leaves a burning crater: steer around it or jump it. Jumps are longer and floatier out here.

    The Lab

    Where new ideas get tried out before they go into the real game. Open it from the title screen, switch on what you want to test, and take a run. Lab runs play by Warp Run’s rules, stay off the world leaderboard, and end by asking for a thumbs up or down.

    Experiment: Jump
    Jump without a ramp. Choose how in the Lab: flick your steering thumb up, or tap with a second finger (Space on a computer). It’s a little bigger than a ramp jump and clears the same things: mines, walls, lasers, trucks, craters and ram bikes. Pylons and crystals are too tall. The lime arrows in the top bar are your jump charges.
    Hold 2 · every 2 close calls earn one back (or switch to unlimited) · AIR +150 per thing you fly over
    Experiment: Sky ramps
    A big lime ramp throws you into a long, floaty flight with five rings in the sky. Steer through them: the brightest one is next. The road underneath is kept clear, so you can also stay down and skip it.
    Rings +200, +400, +600, +800, +1,000 · all five: +1,000, 2 power cells and a jump charge
    Experiment: Hyperlane
    Every world gate drops you into a hyperspace tunnel for about 16 seconds before the next world. No gravity: drag up and down as well as left and right (arrow keys or WASD on a computer). Shoot or dodge the rocks, slip past the red lasers, fly through the hole in each wall, thread the rings, and grab the green cells and the power-up orb. Two flying styles to try: Barrel rolls (it rolls to face where it’s heading and a fast swipe spins it right round) or Smooth banks (always the right way up).
    Rocks +60 · rings +150, +300, ... · all six: +800 and 2 cells · the road waits for you while you’re in the lane
    Test setup
    For trying things at speed without playing for 20 minutes first. Start on a later loop (faster, tougher, score multiplied) and switch on Fully loaded to begin with guns at level 8 and 2 shields. With the hyperlane on, Start in a hyperlane drops you straight into one.

    Score

    Points
    You score for distance (faster = more), plus points for everything you destroy and AIR bonuses. Each mode has its own world leaderboard, which keeps the top 50 runs, and one player can hold several spots. Share a run to challenge a friend.

    Put it on your Home Screen

    Speeder installs like an app: full screen, its own icon, and it works with no signal. It’s free and there’s no app store.

    iPhone and iPad
    1. Open the game in Safari (Chrome works too).
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow). On newer iPhones it’s inside the ••• menu next to the address.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see it, tap View More.
    4. Tap Add. Play from the new icon.
    Android
    1. Open the game in Chrome.
    2. Tap INSTALL APP on the title screen if you see it.
    3. Otherwise tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
    Opened from TikTok, Instagram and the like?
    Those apps show links in their own built-in browser, which can’t install anything. Tap their ••• or share menu and choose Open in browser (or Open in Safari), then follow the steps above. No such option? Copy the link and paste it into Safari or Chrome.
